# Ratewatch #1: The Invisible Enemy *(pitch draft)*

**Premise:** a broker presents a three-week-old book with zero claims as "the cleanest book you'll ever see".
The team goes to war with IBNR.
**Actuarial point:** zero reported claims ≠ zero claims. Claims are incurred but not reported (IBNR), and chain ladder estimates them.
**Layout:** 6 panels, 2 × 3.

Alternatives for key lines are listed underneath. The first one is my pick.

---

**Panel 1:** Command center at night. The big screen shows `NEW BUSINESS: NORDIC WAREHOUSES — CLAIMS TO DATE: 0` in green. Broker Prime grins on a video call.
- BROKER: "Zero claims! Cleanest book you'll ever see."
- GENERAL *(leaning in, unimpressed)*: "It's been live for three weeks."

**Panel 2:** The General briefs the team like a war room and jabs at the screen. Nils sits calmly with his fingers steepled.
- GENERAL: "Zero claims isn't a clean book. It's an ambush."
- NILS *(serene)*: "He's right. The claims exist. They just haven't told us yet."
  - alt: "The claims are there. They're just shy."

**Panel 3:** Close-up of A² on her phone, horrified and delighted. Sudo in the background, not looking up from her laptop.
- A²: "So the claims are ghosting us."
- SUDO: "Statistically, yes."

**Panel 4:** The office lights are off. The General wears **night-vision goggles** and sweeps a flashlight under the desks. Nils is unbothered in the glow of his monitor.
- GENERAL: "Then we hunt them."
- NILS: "They're not in the office, sir. They're in 2027."
  - alt: "Sir, they're not under the desk. They're under development."

**Panel 5:** Nils at the whiteboard with a completed loss triangle and a big circled **47**. A² next to him.
- NILS: "I've developed the triangle. There are 47. One is a flood."
- A²: "So chain ladder is a séance with Excel."
- NILS *(genuinely delighted)*: "…That's actually an excellent summary."

**Panel 6:** Yellow caption: `18 MONTHS LATER`. The screen shows `REPORTED CLAIMS: 46`. The General is triumphant, Nils sips from his BAYES OR DIE mug, and the rubber duck sits on the desk.
- GENERAL: "Forty-six. The Oracle was wrong."
- NILS: "Forty-seven. The flood is running late."
  - alt button: BROKER *(tiny, on a phone screen)*: "…So about the renewal discount?" / GENERAL: "Fire."
