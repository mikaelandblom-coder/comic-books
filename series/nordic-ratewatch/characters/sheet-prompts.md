# Ratewatch character sheet prompts

These sheets become the **visual source of truth** for every episode. Generate each one, pick the best
result, save it here with the filename given below, and attach it to every episode prompt that character appears in.

## How to run them
1. **Use a new ChatGPT chat for each character**, so features from one character don't leak into the next.
2. **Attach the legacy reference image** listed with each prompt. The prompt tells ChatGPT to take only the *face* from it and follow the text for everything else, because the legacy costumes are inconsistent.
3. Paste the prompt block exactly as written.
4. Check the result against the **checklist** under the prompt. Fix small problems with ChatGPT's select-and-edit tool rather than regenerating, because regenerating changes the face.
5. When you're happy, download it and save it as `series/nordic-ratewatch/characters/<file>.png`.
6. After all four are done, run the **line-up** prompt at the bottom. It checks the heights and colours against each other.

The prompts reuse the style lock and character locks from `bible.md`. If you edit a lock in the bible, update it here too.

## 1. Martin Bayesson → `martin.png`
**Attach:** `legacy/ratewatch-02-broker-prime-and-the-jpeg-of-doom.png` (Martin is the blond man in the navy sweater).

```text

light-grey background, so colours are shown accurately.

REFERENCE: The attached comic shows this character (the blond man in the navy sweater). Match
his FACE and HAIR from the reference. Follow the text below for everything else.

CHARACTER:
MARTIN: Swedish man, early 30s, the TALLEST of the team (about 190 cm), slim, short neat blond
hair with a side part, clean-shaven, light Nordic skin, calm symmetrical face, slight polite
smile. Wears a NAVY CREWNECK SWEATER over a LIGHT-BLUE COLLARED SHIRT (collar visible), dark-grey
chinos, brown leather shoes. Signature poses: fingers steepled under his chin, or eyes serenely
closed. Never looks angry; at most mildly puzzled. His mug says "BAYES OR DIE".

LAYOUT (1536×1024 landscape):
- Title at top: "MARTIN BAYESSON — MODEL SHEET"
- Left two-thirds: the same character three times, full body, standing, side by side:
  FRONT view, THREE-QUARTER view, SIDE PROFILE. Neutral standing pose, arms relaxed.
- Right third: a 2×2 grid of head-and-shoulders EXPRESSIONS, each with a tiny label:
  "SERENE" (eyes closed, fingers steepled under chin),
  "HELPFUL" (polite smile, one finger raised as if explaining),
  "MILDLY PUZZLED" (head slightly tilted, one eyebrow up),
  "DELIGHTED" (genuine happy smile, holding a black mug that reads "BAYES OR DIE").
- Bottom strip: small colour swatches labelled SKIN, HAIR, SWEATER, SHIRT, CHINOS, SHOES.

RULES: Identical face, hair and clothing in every view. No other text. No background scenery.
```

**Checklist**
- [ ] Martin is blond with a side part and clean-shaven, with no glasses.
- [ ] He wears a navy sweater, and the light-blue collar is visible.
- [ ] The "SERENE" expression has his eyes closed and fingers steepled.
- [ ] The mug reads "BAYES OR DIE", spelled correctly.

## 5. Team line-up → `lineup.png` (do this last)
**Attach:** all four finished sheets (`martin.png`, `general-cpa.png`, `anika.png`, `zuzana.png`).

```text

ART STYLE: Semi-realistic graphic-novel art with detailed ink linework and cross-hatching.
Even, neutral studio lighting on a plain light-grey background with faint horizontal HEIGHT

LAYOUT (1536×1024 landscape), four characters standing side by side, full body, facing the
viewer, each with their name in a small caption under their feet:
1. "MARTIN" — about 190 cm, tallest, fingers steepled, serene.
2. "GENERAL C.P.A." — about 178 cm but the broadest, arms crossed, scowling.
3. "ANIKA" — about 160 cm, shortest, bubble tea in hand, side-eye toward the General.
4. "ZUZANA" — about 168 cm, laptop under arm, bored.

Title at top: "THE NORDIC RATEWATCH — TEAM LINE-UP". No other text.
```

**Checklist**
- [ ] Heights go Martin > General > Zuzana > Anika, and the difference is visible.
- [ ] You can tell who's who from colour alone: navy/light blue, olive, purple, black.
